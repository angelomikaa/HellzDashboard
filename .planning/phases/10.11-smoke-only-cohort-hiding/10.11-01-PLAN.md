# Phase 10.11 Plan 01 — Smoke-Only Cohort Hiding

**Requirements:** SHWC-03
**Depends on:** Phase 10 (Início showcase), Phase 10.2 (per-build carousels), Phase 10.3 (meta cohorts + primers), Phase 10.10 (per-build gate)
**Migration:** `0055_inicio_placeholder_flag.sql`

## Goal

A build with no real player behind it must not occupy the Início rotation with
fake cards. The moment a real member joins that cohort, the build returns by
itself. Builds with at least one real player keep their placeholder fillers.

## Tasks

1. **Migration 0055** — recreate `get_promotion()` and `get_promotion_public()`
   from their LIVE bodies with one addition: `is_placeholder` =
   `coalesce(m.discord_handle like 'smoke%', false)` in `class_members`,
   propagated through `ovr_rows`/`top3`, serialised as `'placeholder'` on each
   card. Grants/bookkeeping/`notify pgrst` preserved.

2. **Client type** — `PromotionCardRow.placeholder?: boolean` (optional for
   deploy-order safety; absent = real).

3. **Selection helper** — `selectShowcaseCards` in `buildGroups.ts`:
   - group by `char_function`;
   - a build with no `placeholder !== true` card is dropped;
   - otherwise keep platinum cards, or all cards when the build has no platinum.

4. **Tests** — all-placeholder platinum build dropped (Algoz), real +
   placeholders kept (Mestre-Ferreiro), smoke-only sibling hidden while the
   real sibling stays, real gold + filler fallback, missing flag = real.

## Success criteria

1. Assassin Cross dps/mdps (and every other smoke-only build) disappear from Início.
2. A build with a real member keeps rendering, including its placeholder fillers.
3. A real member appearing in a hidden cohort makes it render on the next fetch,
   with no data change beyond that member's normal submission.
4. The highlights closed field list is unchanged; no client rank math.
5. `tsc -b`, the full suite, `vite build` and `smoke-promotion` stay green.
