## Remaining limitations / recommendations

1. **First submission + small cohort is inherently weak**: without a previous validated row and with n<5, the population curve cannot separate a buff from a legitimate build. PaiDeMenina's PATK% 96.26 is inside the Artista cohort's natural spread; only MDEF% was anomalous. A first-time high variance record is a legitimate INCONCLUSIVE case.
2. **Seed realism**: to test the Angelus/Assumptio curve, seed the buff on the **defense flat** too (`rpdef`/`rmdef` ×1.95, or the Equipment PDEF/MDEF raw), not only on the equipment percentage. The current seed is a strong percentage signature and is now caught by the history reference, but the flat curve stays normal.
3. **Dashboard surface**: pending rows never appear in the regression view by design. Use Validações for pending anti-cheat evidence; the dashboard is for validated history/analysis.
4. A cohort-level fallback (guild-wide) was not re-introduced; the self-history reference is the chosen solution for small cohorts.
