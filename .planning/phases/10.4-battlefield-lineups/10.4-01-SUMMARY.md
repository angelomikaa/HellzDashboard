## Device guard (0041)

The `0024` device-binding design was never applied live: `assert_device_bound()` and `request_device_fingerprint()` did not exist, so every lineup write failed with SQLSTATE 42883. Owner decision: migration `0041_device_binding_guard.sql` creates only those two functions, with the 0024 bodies, grants and comments. The guard compares the `X-Device-Fingerprint` header with the JWT `device_fp` claim and fails closed on either side being empty.

Per the owner's instruction, `0024_device_binding_enforcement.sql` was **not** applied. Its stub bodies for `set_member_role`, `set_member_active`, `reset_member_pin` and `add_member` remain untouched. Those four staff RPCs still do not call the guard; enforcing it on them is a separate decision.
