## Implementation Decisions (Locked with Owner 2026-10-30)

### D-01: Tab Access & Permissions (Member Read + Officer Edit)
- **Visibility:** All active logged-in members see the **Lineup** tab in the main navigation bar (placed right after `Enviar Status` so every member can check their party and download the raid JPGs).
- **Member capabilities:** Read-only view of both battlefields (**Vigrid Field — Raid 1** and **Ruinscape — Raid 2**), automatic visual highlight on the member's own slot/party ("Sua PT"), and full access to the JPG download buttons.
- **Officer / Owner capabilities:** Full inline editing — assign any active real member (non-smoke) to any slot (`G1`–`G8`, slots `1`–`5`), swap/move members across slots or raids, clear a slot (for when the guild has < 80 active players), and edit the optional party assignment header (e.g. `MOBS CIMA/BAIXO`, `MOBS DIREITA/ESQUERDA`).
- **Server enforcement:** All writes go through `SECURITY DEFINER` RPCs (`set_lineup_slot`, `set_lineup_party_label`, `swap_lineup_slots`) gated by `auth.jwt()->>'member_role' in ('officer', 'owner')`, active-member check, and `public.assert_device_bound()` (0024 discipline). Direct table writes (`INSERT`/`UPDATE`/`DELETE`) are revoked from `anon` and `authenticated`.

### D-03: Party Structure, Leader Slot & Editable Party Labels
- **2 Battlefields:**
  - `'vigrid_field'` → **Vigrid Field (Raid 1)**
  - `'ruinscape'` → **Ruinscape (Raid 2)**
- **8 Parties per Battlefield:** `party_index` `1`..`8` (`G1`..`G8`).
- **Up to 5 Slots per Party:** `slot_index` `1`..`5`.
  - **Slot 1 = Party Leader:** Always styled with bold typography, a subtle leader crown/badge (`LÍDER`), and a stronger accent border in both the web UI and the exported JPG.
  - **Empty slots allowed:** When a slot has `member_id = NULL`, it renders as an empty slot (`— Vago —`) and the party footer counter dynamically reflects filled slots (`0/5`..`5/5`, highlighted in green when `5/5` and amber when `< 5/5`).
- **Editable Party Role Label:** Each party (`battlefield`, `party_index`) has an optional `role_label` (`text`, max 40 chars).
  - Default seeded labels:
    - Vigrid Field (Raid 1): `G5` = `'MOBS CIMA/BAIXO'`, `G6` = `'MOBS DIREITA/ESQUERDA'`
    - Ruinscape (Raid 2): `G3` = `'MOBS CIMA/BAIXO'`, `G4` = `'MOBS DIREITA/ESQUERDA'`
  - Officers can edit or clear any party's `role_label` inline (with quick presets for `MOBS CIMA/BAIXO`, `MOBS DIREITA/ESQUERDA`, or custom text).

### D-04: Smoke Exclusion & Single-Slot Uniqueness
- **Smokes never count:** Any member row where `discord_handle ilike 'smoke%'` or `discord_handle ilike 'dummy-%'` or `active = false` is excluded from `get_lineups()`'s assignable roster and rejected by `set_lineup_slot()` (`raise exception 'smoke_or_inactive_member'`).
- **One slot per player:** `lineup_slots.member_id` carries a `UNIQUE` constraint across all battlefields and parties. When an officer selects a player who is already assigned to another party/raid, `set_lineup_slot()` atomically vacates their old slot (or swaps if requested) and places them in the target slot — zero duplicate players across Raid 1 and Raid 2.
