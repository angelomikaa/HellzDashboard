## Owner correction 2026-11-06 — real-guild references

Owner report: "o maior dano médio da guild entre os arquimagos é Venon, mas o
gráfico parece não condizer". Root cause was the fixture scope, not the math:
`my_stat_progress` averaged every active member, including the sanctioned
`smoke%` fixtures. For High Wizard / `dmg_vs_medium` the four smoke rows
(26.6 / 25.2 / 1.9 / 1.8) dragged the class average from **40.69** to **27.28**
and the cohort from **40.69** to **35.76**.

The same pollution existed in other displayed averages/lists:

| Surface | Before (with fixtures) | After |
|---|---|---|
| Tracker Classe / Função / Coorte | 27.28 / 27.57 / 35.76 | **40.69 / 36.80 / 40.69** |
| Painel StatCard "Média · Melhor" (`my_report`) | fixture-inclusive | real-only |
| Ranking "Ver stats" `class_average` (`role_rank`) | fixture-inclusive | real-only |
| Ranking OVR board + Stats-Chave top-5 rows | included smoke players | real players only |

Migrations:

- **0075** `my_stat_progress`: the three references exclude `smoke%`/`dummy-%`;
  the caller's own value/history are untouched.
- **0076** `my_report` + `role_rank`: a second `agg_real` feeds ONLY the
  displayed `class_average`/`best`; points/OVR/`data_sufficient` keep the 0073
  all-active basis, so no OVR moved and the six smoke-primed cohorts with one
  real member keep their rating.
- **0077** `role_rank` + `class_top5`: the output rows hide fixtures; the OVR
  lateral still reads the all-active `agg` (parity preserved).

Live verification: ZekRAIZAX Nr / `dmg_vs_medium` now reports class 40.69,
function 36.80, cohort 40.69, own 30, best 70.26 (Venon), points 42.27 and OVR
93.483 — identical to before for points/OVR. The High Wizard board/top-5 list
returns Atillos, Hard, PeterAxe, Venon and ZekRAIZAX Nr, zero `Smoke%` rows.

Chart detail: when two references are equal (class and cohort often share the
same members) their labels are nudged apart instead of overprinting.
